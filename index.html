Preparing your home

Homes sell on how they feel.

Buyers form an opinion online long before they walk in. A little preparation changes that first impression, and I'll organise it for you.

  • Online firstNearly every buyer starts on a screen. Bright, well-shot rooms are what earn the showing.
  • Easy to pictureSimple staging shows how each room works and how much space there really is.
  • Better offersA home that looks cared for gives buyers confidence, and confident buyers pay more.

Selling

A plan for any market.

There's no one-size plan. I'll look at your street and the market the week we list, recommend a strategy and explain exactly why.

When buyers are competing

Create urgency, then choose the best offer.

When homes are moving fast, the goal is to bring as many qualified buyers to the table at the same time as possible.

  1. The right list pricePriced to attract the largest group of qualified buyers, using fresh comparable sales.
  2. A quiet launch, then a big debutA heads-up to my buyer list and local agents in the days before the listing goes live.
  3. A clear offer dateWhen it suits the market, a set date for offers gives every buyer time to view and get ready.
  4. Competition works for youWith several buyers interested, each offer is used to improve price, deposit and conditions.
  5. Early offers, handled wellIf a buyer tries to jump the line, we weigh it against what offer day is likely to bring.
  6. Calm to the finishSteady negotiation on price, closing date and inclusions until everything is signed.

When buyers have choices

Stand out, then negotiate with patience.

When listings sit longer, buyers take their time. Your home needs to look like the best option, with a clear pricing strategy behind it.

  1. Be the best on the streetPreparation, staging and photography count for even more when buyers are comparing.
  2. Price with a strategyA list price with room built in and limits agreed ahead of time, so every counter is planned.
  3. Follow up every showingFeedback from every buyer's agent, so we know exactly what's working and what isn't.
  4. Defend your valueCounter-offers that protect your price instead of giving it away a little at a time.
  5. Terms that keep the dealDeposits, conditions and dates written so a firm deal stays firm.
  6. Decide with dataAny change is based on showings, feedback and new sales, never on worry.

Buying instead? I bring the same strategy to your side of the table, so you know what the seller is likely to do next.
