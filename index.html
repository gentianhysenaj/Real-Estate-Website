I'm Gentian. I help buyers and sellers across the Greater Toronto Area make one of the biggest decisions of their lives with clear advice, honest numbers and a lot of local knowledge.

Buying or selling a home in the GTA moves fast. My job is to slow it down just enough for you to make a confident decision. I'll walk you through the market, the neighbourhoods and the numbers, and I'll tell you what I'd do in your shoes.

Whether it's your first condo, a move up for a growing family or selling the house you've loved for years, you'll get quick answers, careful negotiation and no pressure.
